# UltraWear FC Fan Experience Foundation

## Product principle

Design for fans first. Search traffic and monetization must support the product, not overwhelm it.

## Primary navigation

`Home`, `Live`, `Scores`, `News`, `Sports`, `Explore`, `Community`, `Me`.

## Core experiences

### Home
Dynamic feed showing live events, important stories, followed teams, upcoming matches, trending topics and useful discovery.

### Live/Scores
Real-time scores, match status, event timelines and sport filters.

### Match Centre
Teams, score, status, timeline, statistics, lineups, standings context, form, prediction, related news and post-match meaning.

### Entities
Every important team, player, competition and match has a canonical page powered by the sports data model.

### Personalization
Users can follow sports, competitions, teams, players and topics. Anonymous preferences should work before account creation; authenticated users can synchronize preferences.

### Fan participation
Polls, predictions, quizzes, reactions, saved stories, streaks and community discussions. Prediction features must remain non-gambling fan participation unless separately licensed and approved.

### Notifications
Users control categories and frequency. Support match start, goals, final result and important breaking news without notification spam.

## UX retention loop

```text
discover -> consume -> follow -> personalize -> participate -> receive useful update -> return
```

Retention must come from usefulness and community, not dark patterns, misleading notifications or forced interactions.

## AI assistance

Use contextual AI to explain verified platform data: what happened, why a result matters, who is trending and what comes next. Clearly distinguish facts, inference, prediction and opinion.

## Accessibility and performance

Use semantic HTML, keyboard support, screen-reader support, accessible controls, responsive layouts and strong Core Web Vitals. Media and advertising must not degrade the core experience.
