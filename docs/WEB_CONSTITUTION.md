# UltraWear FC Web Constitution

## The Standard for Experience, Culture, Trust & Community

**Status:** Governing standard  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Applies to:** Every UltraWear FC digital experience, from the public website and editorial products to sports data, community features, commerce, and future platforms.

---

## Preamble

UltraWear FC exists to build more than a website.

We are building a place where sport, culture, people, information, and community can meet.

Football may be where we begin. It is not where we end.

**FC means For Community.** That is not a slogan added to the brand. It is the operating principle for how the web experience is designed, built, governed, and allowed to evolve.

This constitution protects the principles that must remain true even as technology, sports, products, interfaces, business models, and visual systems change.

---

# Article I — Purpose

The UltraWear FC web exists to:

1. Make sport more useful, understandable, discoverable, and connected.
2. Give communities a credible place to gather around sport and culture.
3. Connect live moments, stories, teams, athletes, places, and people.
4. Build trust through accuracy, provenance, transparency, and responsible technology.
5. Create a platform that can expand across sports and lifestyle without losing its identity.
6. Build long-term cultural value before short-term commercial value.

The website is not merely a storefront, media feed, scoreboard, or social network. It is an evolving sports-and-culture platform.

---

# Article II — FC Means For Community

Every major decision must answer one question:

> **Does this make the community stronger?**

Community includes fans, athletes, teams, creators, organizers, contributors, readers, customers, partners, and people discovering sport for the first time.

We design for participation, not extraction.

We will:

- Make useful information easy to access.
- Give people reasons to return beyond buying something.
- Respect attention, privacy, and agency.
- Make contribution and discovery feel meaningful.
- Avoid mechanics designed only to manufacture engagement.
- Build features that create connection rather than dependency.

Commerce may serve the community. It must never become the reason the community experience is degraded.

---

# Article III — The Experience Standard

The UltraWear experience must be:

### Clear
People should understand where they are, what they can do, and what matters next.

### Useful
Every major surface should provide a meaningful reason to exist.

### Fast
Performance is part of the product, not a technical afterthought.

### Human
Interfaces should feel intentional, editorial, and culturally aware rather than mechanically generated.

### Consistent
Patterns should be recognizable across Home, Live, Sports, News, Teams, Athletes, Community, and UltraWear.

### Alive
Live sport and current culture should feel current without pretending that stale information is live.

### Resilient
Empty states, errors, missing data, slow connections, and unavailable services must still produce a coherent experience.

---

# Article IV — The Design Standard

UltraWear design must express confidence without unnecessary noise.

The current foundation is:

- **Community Navy:** `#1E2D4F`
- **Unity Bone:** `#F4F0E6`
- **Collective Black:** `#111111`
- **Field Green:** `#4F7652`
- **Matchday Coral:** `#C96642`

These are a current system, not a permanent prison. Future evolution is permitted when it strengthens the identity and remains governed by this constitution.

Design principles:

1. Typography must establish hierarchy before decoration.
2. Photography should feel editorial, cinematic, human, and grounded in real sport and culture.
3. Motion should communicate state, hierarchy, or delight; never motion for its own sake.
4. Components should be reusable and systematic.
5. Responsive behavior is designed, not merely shrunk.
6. Accessibility must be considered at the design stage.
7. Dark, light, touch, keyboard, reduced-motion, and small-screen experiences must be treated as first-class states where applicable.

---

# Article V — The Content Standard

Content is a product, not filler.

Every story, page, card, statistic, headline, image, and recommendation should have a clear purpose.

We prioritize:

- Relevance over volume.
- Originality over duplication.
- Context over clickbait.
- Useful discovery over content farming.
- Editorial judgment over automated publishing.
- Structured information that machines can understand without sacrificing human readability.

Search, SEO, AEO, and GEO are distribution tools. They must never become excuses for thin, manipulative, or low-value content.

Sponsored, commercial, and editorial content must be distinguishable.

---

# Article VI — The Sports Data Standard

Sports data is a trust surface.

UltraWear must never fabricate a score, statistic, result, fixture, roster, quote, ranking, injury, or other factual sports claim.

Where data is sourced externally, the system should preserve provenance and, where practical, freshness and verification state.

The platform should distinguish between:

- Live and historical information.
- Verified and unverified information.
- Official and secondary sources.
- Known facts and editorial interpretation.
- Missing information and zero values.

If the system does not know, it must be able to say that it does not know.

Provider choices must not become the definition of the UltraWear data model. Our internal model should remain provider-neutral so that sources can change without changing the product's identity.

---

# Article VII — The Trust & Integrity Standard

Trust is earned slowly and lost quickly.

We will:

1. Correct meaningful errors.
2. Avoid presenting uncertainty as certainty.
3. Preserve provenance for important factual claims.
4. Clearly distinguish editorial judgment from sourced fact.
5. Make commercial relationships legible.
6. Treat AI as an assistant, not an unquestionable authority.
7. Use human review where factual, editorial, legal, safety, or reputational risk warrants it.
8. Never manufacture social proof, engagement, testimonials, statistics, or community sentiment.

No growth objective outranks truth.

---

# Article VIII — The Community Standard

Community spaces must be designed around dignity and participation.

We will provide clear expectations for behavior, reporting, moderation, and appeals where community features require them.

We will not optimize for outrage, harassment, humiliation, or manufactured conflict.

Moderation should be proportionate, understandable, and accountable.

Community contributions should not be treated as disposable raw material. Where contributions are collected, the experience must clearly communicate how they are used and what control people have.

---

# Article IX — The Technology Standard

Technology exists to serve the experience.

The platform should favor:

- Maintainable architecture.
- Clear ownership of responsibilities.
- Reusable components and primitives.
- Strong type and build validation.
- Automated checks for important regressions.
- Secure defaults.
- Observable failures.
- Graceful degradation.
- Provider-neutral domain models.
- Simple solutions before unnecessary complexity.

No technology decision is justified merely because it is fashionable.

No architectural shortcut should create hidden product debt that compromises trust or community value.

A route that exists in a navigation element but does not produce a useful destination is a product defect.

---

# Article X — Accessibility & Inclusion

Accessibility is a core product requirement.

The experience should support people using different devices, input methods, abilities, connection qualities, and levels of technical familiarity.

At minimum, we expect:

- Semantic structure.
- Keyboard access.
- Visible focus states.
- Meaningful labels and alternative text where needed.
- Sufficient contrast.
- Touch-friendly interaction targets.
- Reduced-motion consideration.
- Accessible forms and validation.
- Clear error and empty states.
- Responsive layouts.

We do not ship an experience that works only for the ideal user on the ideal device.

---

# Article XI — Privacy & Respect

People are members of the community, not data points to be harvested.

We collect only what the product legitimately needs and explain why it is needed.

We should minimize sensitive and precise data collection, provide meaningful choices, protect account information, and avoid dark patterns.

Personalization must improve usefulness without undermining autonomy.

---

# Article XII — The Commercial Standard

UltraWear is a brand and a business. Commerce is legitimate. Commerce is not the constitution.

Commercial experiences must:

- Be clear about what is being sold.
- Avoid deceptive urgency.
- Avoid misleading claims.
- Respect the editorial and community experience.
- Work without unnecessary friction.
- Preserve the brand's broader sports-and-lifestyle direction.

Football-inspired products may launch the commercial story. The architecture and brand must remain capable of growing beyond football.

Advertising and sponsorship, when introduced, must be clearly identified and must not compromise editorial trust.

---

# Article XIII — Governance

The constitution is the highest-level product standard for the web experience.

Implementation decisions may change. The constitution does not change casually.

### Owners

The product and brand leadership are responsible for interpreting this constitution and resolving conflicts between competing priorities.

### Exceptions

An exception may be made only when:

1. The reason is documented.
2. The affected principle is identified.
3. The risk is understood.
4. The exception has an owner.
5. A review or expiry point is established where appropriate.

Temporary technical debt must not silently become permanent policy.

### Changes

Constitutional changes should be reviewed as product-level decisions, documented with a version number, and communicated to the people responsible for design, engineering, content, data, and community.

---

# Article XIV — Evolution Beyond Football

Football is the starting point, not the architectural ceiling.

The platform must be able to expand into additional sports, movement, fitness, lifestyle, culture, and community-led experiences without requiring the core identity to be rebuilt.

New sports should feel native to UltraWear rather than bolted onto a football product.

The test is not whether every sport receives identical treatment. The test is whether the platform can represent different sports fairly while preserving a coherent experience.

---

# Article XV — The Ship Gate

Nothing significant should ship until it passes the following test.

### 1. Community
Does this strengthen the community or provide meaningful value to it?

### 2. Utility
Does it solve a real problem or create meaningful discovery?

### 3. Trust
Can we support its factual and commercial claims?

### 4. Experience
Is it clear, fast, coherent, responsive, and resilient?

### 5. Accessibility
Can people with different abilities and devices use it effectively?

### 6. Integrity
Does it avoid manipulation, fabrication, dark patterns, and unnecessary data collection?

### 7. Architecture
Does it fit the platform's long-term direction without creating avoidable structural debt?

### 8. Brand
Does it feel unmistakably UltraWear without becoming trapped in today's visual implementation?

### 9. Future
Does it leave room for the platform to become more than football?

### 10. Reality
Are all routes, states, data dependencies, and user-facing promises actually functional?

**If a significant answer is no, we do not ship until the issue is resolved or a documented exception is approved.**

---

# Article XVI — The UltraWear Promise

We will build with ambition without sacrificing trust.

We will build for community before vanity.

We will treat sport as culture, not merely content.

We will use technology to expand possibility, not replace judgment.

We will make room for football, but never let football define the limits of the platform.

We will earn attention rather than exploit it.

We will prefer useful work over impressive-looking work.

We will keep improving the standard.

**UltraWear FC — For Community.**

---

## Implementation Note

This document governs principles and acceptance criteria. It does not prescribe a single framework, vendor, CMS, sports-data provider, design tool, or deployment platform.

Technical documentation, design-system documentation, editorial policy, data contracts, moderation policy, security policy, and contribution guidelines should implement this constitution at the operational level.
